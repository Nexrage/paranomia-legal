Paranomia Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 16, 2026

Paranomia is made by Nexrage Studios. This policy explains how the iOS game handles information.

We do not collect personal data

  • No accounts or sign-in
  • No analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs
  • No access to contacts, location, camera, microphone, or photos
  • No third-party ads or in-app purchases

What stays on your device

The game stores progress locally: how far you have gone, whether he found life, and a few tutorial flags. That data never leaves the device.

Children

We do not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.
